# Q-030: Regular groceries with a frequency, and suggestions (T-109, T-110)
Raised by: orchestrator / tasks T-109, T-110
Status: answered

## Context
Architect request 2026-11-05: weekly groceries get a frequency (every week, every second week, monthly), and the app learns from items added by hand (not from recipes) to suggest regular groceries. See T-109 and T-110.

## 1. What "monthly" means
1. **The first shopping week of each month** (the week whose Monday falls in the month, or the first week that contains the 1st; the app shows the next due week so it is never a surprise).
2. Every fourth week from a start week (simple, but drifts against calendar months, 13 times a year).

Recommendation: 1 (matches "once a month" as people think of it).

## 2. Which weeks "every second week" lands on
1. **Counted from the week the item was added or its frequency was changed; the household page shows "next: week 47", and the user can move it to "this week" or "next week".**
2. Odd or even ISO weeks.

Recommendation: 1.

## 3. Should the button add due items automatically
1. **No: still one tap "Add regular groceries" (it adds what is due this week).** Automatic adding can come later.
2. Due items are added automatically when the week's list is first created.

Recommendation: 1 (keeps today's behaviour, nothing appears unasked).

## 4. Learning: how much history and dismissing
1. **Look at the last 12 weeks of lists; suggest when a hand-added item appears in at least 4 of them; a dismissed suggestion stays hidden for 12 weeks (stored per household, a small table, so T-110 gets a migration).**
2. Same, but dismissing is only for this session (no migration).

Recommendation: 1.

## 5. The name
1. **"Regular groceries" (nb "Faste varer")** instead of "Weekly groceries".
2. Keep "Weekly groceries".

Recommendation: 1.

## Answer (architect)
Answered 2026-11-06: option 1 on all five points.
1. Monthly = the first shopping week of each month, not every fourth week. Orchestrator's precision (the question left two wordings): the first week whose Monday falls in that month, so every month has exactly one due week.
2. Every second week counts from when the item was added or its frequency changed; the user can move it to this week or next week.
3. The button stays; nothing is added unasked.
4. Learning: last 12 weeks, suggest at 4 or more of them, a dismissed suggestion stays hidden for 12 weeks (stored per household, so T-110 has a migration).
5. "Regular groceries" (nb "Faste varer"). Orchestrator's reading: this renames the UI text; the API routes and table stay `weekly-groceries`/`WeeklyGroceries` to avoid churn in the contract and cached clients.
