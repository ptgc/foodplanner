# Q-029: "What can I make with what we have?" (T-100)
Raised by: orchestrator / task T-100
Status: answered

## Context
T-100 suggests recipes for today from what the pantry (T-093) holds right now: first recipes the household can cook fully, then recipes missing only a few things ("missing: ..."). Already settled by earlier decisions: to-taste, as-needed, optional lines and small frying fat never count as missing; a line with alternatives is satisfied if any alternative is at home; staples count as at home; expired pantry entries do not count. Four open points.

## 1. How amounts count
1. **A pantry entry without an amount means "we have some" and satisfies any amount. With an amount, it must cover the recipe amount at the chosen servings (same dimension; mass and volume are not converted into each other unless the ingredient has a density); not enough counts as missing ("missing: 200 g more cheese").**
2. Ignore amounts: any pantry entry for the ingredient satisfies the line.

Recommendation: 1 (uses the T-093 amounts the family already enters).

## 2. How many missing items still show
1. **Show recipes missing at most 2 things (not counting the rules above); more than that is not "from what we have".**
2. At most 3.
3. Make it a setting.

Recommendation: 1.

## 3. Ranking within each group
1. **Fewest missing first, then the kids' likes (T-026), then not cooked in the last 14 days, then name.** The week's meal mix rules (T-090) are not applied (this is a "today" helper), but the food type is shown.
2. Fewest missing, then name only.

Recommendation: 1.

## 4. Picking a suggestion
1. **One tap "Cook today" adds it to today's plan (Adult/Owner; in kid mode the screen is not shown), and the missing items can be added to this week's shopping list with a second tap.**
2. View only; the user adds it to the plan the usual way.

Recommendation: 1.

## Answer (architect)
Answered 2026-11-08: option 1 on all four points (as recommended).
1. A pantry entry without an amount satisfies any amount; with an amount it must cover the recipe amount at the chosen servings (same dimension, no mass/volume conversion without a density); a shortfall counts as missing.
2. Show recipes missing at most 2 things.
3. Fewest missing, then the kids' likes, then not cooked in the last 14 days, then name; meal mix rules not applied; food type shown.
4. One tap "Cook today" adds it to today's plan (Adult/Owner; hidden in kid mode); a second tap adds the missing items to this week's shopping list.
