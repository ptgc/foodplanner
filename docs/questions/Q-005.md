# Q-005: Invite model: shareable link or email-bound, and which roles it may grant
Raised by: spec-writer / task T-011 (also affects T-010, T-045)
Status: answered

## Context
T-011 adds invite create and accept (single-use, expiring), and T-010 carries the invite token through the Google round trip. Neither the task nor ADR 0004/0005 says who may accept an invite. It could be anyone holding the link, or only the person whose email was invited. They also do not say which roles an invite may grant. Domain says the Owner manages members. T-044 implies a household can have more than one Owner ("last Owner cannot leave without transferring ownership"). No task sends email (see Q-002).

## Options
1. **Shareable bearer link.** The Owner creates an invite for role Adult or Child and gets a URL to share by any channel (SMS, WhatsApp). The first authenticated user to accept it joins. It is single-use, expires after a configurable time (default 7 days), and only a hash of the token is stored. Trade-off: no email needed and it fits families, but a leaked link lets a stranger join until it is used or expires.
2. **Email-bound invite.** The Owner enters an email address. Only a user whose confirmed or provider-verified email matches can accept. Single-use and expiry still apply. Trade-off: stronger, but it needs confirmed local emails (Q-002 option 1) and ideally an email sender. It also fails for family members who sign in with a different address than the one the Owner typed.
3. **Bearer link with Owner approval.** As option 1, but accepting creates a pending membership that an Owner must approve. Trade-off: safe against leaked links, but adds a state, endpoints and UI that are not in any task.

Roles, in any option: (a) invites grant Adult or Child only, and ownership comes only from creating a household or by transfer (T-044); or (b) invites may also grant Owner.

## Recommendation
Option 1 with (a). It needs no email infrastructure, matches how families share links, and single-use plus short expiry limits the risk. Keeping Owner out of invites avoids accidental privilege grants.

## Answer (architect)
Decided 2026-10-22: option 1 with (a). An invite is a shareable single-use bearer link, default 7-day expiry, only a hash stored. Any signed-in user holding the token may accept. Invites grant Adult or Child only; Owner comes from creating a household or from transfer (T-044).
