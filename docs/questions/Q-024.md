# Q-024: May Foodplanner act as the MCP client for a household's Oda account?
Raised by: implementer-backend / task T-001
Status: open

## Context
Oda's hosted MCP server (https://oda.com/mcp) is the only supported way found to fill an Oda cart. Its terms (https://oda.com/no/legal/betingelser/#bruk-av-oda-via-ki-assistenter-mcp, excerpt in `spikes/oda/findings/tos-mcp-section.md`) say the user agrees:
- to use it only for their own shopping at Oda (private or own business);
- not to extract, copy or collect products, prices, recipes or other content beyond what their own shopping needs;
- not to use it on behalf of others, nor resell it or give others access;
- not to circumvent usage limits or safeguards.
The same text says "apps and AI assistants" that the user connects are allowed and are provided by others than Oda; the user chooses which apps get access and can revoke it; what an app does counts as the user's own activity. Oda may limit or close access, in serious cases the account.

ADR 0008 (T-053 "Oda cart") would make Foodplanner a third-party app that holds one household member's Oda token and adds items for the household's shared list. Two readings are unclear: (1) is a family app that fills the cart of one adult a connected "app" in the sense of the terms, or "on behalf of others"; (2) does our product search (matching every catalog ingredient to Oda products, possibly caching ids) count as "collecting products or prices". ADR 0004/0008 do not decide this.

## Options
1. Ask Oda in writing (partner / MCP feedback contact) before building T-053. Slowest, but the only definitive answer; the spike's Phase B results can be attached.
2. Build only the "user connects their own Oda account in their own session" flow: the token belongs to one adult, only that person triggers cart fills, no price or product caching beyond the current request (store at most the Oda product id the user picked, per household). Lowest ToS risk; may still be "on behalf of others" for a family list.
3. Do not act as the MCP client at all: assistant-bridge route (Foodplanner exposes the list as text export or its own MCP server; the user's own assistant, already connected to Oda, fills the cart). No Oda credentials in our system; ToS-safe; more manual for the user.

## Recommendation
Option 3 as the product default (already compatible with `IShoppingListExporter`), and option 1 in parallel, because it decides whether option 2 may ever ship. Do not start T-053's direct client until Oda answers. Option 2 only with a written yes.
