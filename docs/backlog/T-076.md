## Goal
Model what "salt etter smak" and "margarin til steking, eller smør/olje" really mean (Q-018), structured (rule 5), filled by the rule-based importer:
- Amount kind per ingredient line: Measured (quantity + unit), ToTaste ("etter smak"), AsNeeded ("til steking", "til pensling" without an amount).
- Purpose per line: Main, Frying, Serving, Garnish, Seasoning (detected from phrases like "til steking/stekning", "til servering", "til pynt", "etter smak"); Optional flag ("evt.", "kan sløyfes").
- Alternatives: "X eller Y" → primary ingredient + alternative catalog ingredients (never free text).

Read CLAUDE.md (rules 3, 5), docs/domain.md (Recipes, Quantities, Shopping list generation, Pantry, Recipe import), docs/questions/Q-017.md and Q-018.md. How an ingredient is used stays in the steps; the ingredient list is the prep list.
