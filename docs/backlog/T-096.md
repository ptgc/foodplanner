## Goal
Architect request 2026-10-29: a system admin user (above the per-household roles Owner/Adult/Child) who can do support work on households: look up a household and its members, fix problems (e.g. a lost Owner, a stuck invite, a locked PIN, bad data), without being a member of every household. Plan first (spec + decisions), then the architect decides when to build it.

## Context
Read CLAUDE.md hard rules 1 and 2 (tenancy filter, policy-based authorization), docs/domain.md (tenancy, ICurrentHousehold, the single tenancy bypass in TenantResolution.cs, memberships, invites, ownership transfer, PIN lockout T-026), ADRs. Crossing tenants is the most sensitive change in the app: it needs an explicit, audited, narrow path, not a general filter bypass.
