## Goal
The kid screens for T-026, for children about 4-8 (many cannot read). Architect decisions 2026-10-28: image-first, big photos and touch targets, icons instead of words, no cooking time and almost no text; kids like dishes (tap a big heart, tap again to unlike); kids make the week plan by picking a dinner for each day, but only among recipes the household rules (T-090) still allow for that day.
