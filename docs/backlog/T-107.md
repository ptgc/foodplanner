## Goal
Architect request 2026-11-02 (stretch): look at Oda's prices and what is on offer this week, and use it to suggest dinners for the week plan (recipes whose main ingredients are cheap or discounted now) and things worth buying (e.g. stock up on a staple the household uses that is on offer). Suggestions only; the family still picks, and the household meal mix rules (T-090) and the pantry (T-093) apply.

## Context and constraints (found in T-001 Phase A, 2026-10-31)
- Oda's MCP manifest (25 tools) has no "offers" or "campaign" tool, and the product schema has `price` and `unitPrice` but no discount field. `get_cart` reports discounts only for what is already in the cart. `likely_to_buy` and `unique_for_you` exist and may be personal recommendations. So offers may not be reachable through MCP at all; Phase B of T-001 should check whether search results show campaign prices.
- Oda's terms (MCP section) forbid extracting or collecting products and prices beyond the user's own shopping. A price list built for suggestions may count as collecting. This task must not start before Q-024 is answered, and it should keep to the user's own shopping: look up prices on demand for the household's own recipes and list, no catalogue-wide crawl, no long-lived price store (or only what Oda allows in writing).
- Alternative route without a direct Oda client (Q-024 option 3): foodplanner exposes its recipes and list; the user's own AI assistant, connected to Oda, asks Oda for offers and suggests dinners.

## Open points (raise a question first)
- Which route (direct client vs assistant bridge) after Q-024.
- What "on offer" means if Oda gives no discount field (price drop vs last week would need stored prices, which the terms may forbid).
- How suggestions are shown: a "good value this week" row in the adult planner; never in kid mode unless the adults want it.
