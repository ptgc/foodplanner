## Decision
Decision (architect, 2026-11-22, after the real-Oda try-out showed first-hit products the household does not buy): do both, history first. T-114 (prefer what the household usually buys, nothing stored) comes first; then T-052, where an Adult picks the preferred product on the Oda result screen from the top search hits that the same cart fill returned (no second Oda sign-in). Priority order in the fill: the household's own pick (T-052), then purchase history (T-114), then the first available hit. No "connection closed" line is wanted on the result screen.

## Goal
When "Add to Oda cart" searches for an item, prefer a search hit the household has bought before at Oda over the first hit, so the usual cherry tomatoes, mayonnaise or cheese go in from the start (architect request 2026-11-20). It happens inside the same session-only connection as T-053, and nothing is stored.

## Context
Read CLAUDE.md, ADR 0008, Q-024, Q-025, Q-032 (answer) and docs/specs/T-053-oda-cart-session.md.
- Oda's MCP tools `likely_to_buy` (products the user usually buys, from purchase history) and `get_orders` / `get_order` (order history with line items) are the household's own shopping data. Using them for the household's own cart is within Oda's terms ("own shopping").
- T-001 Phase B ran `likely_to_buy`: 50 products with the same fields as search, no offer data. `get_orders` and `get_order` were not tried, so their paging, depth and response size are unknown. Check them first and keep the number of calls small (Oda usage limits).
- The history is read during the cart fill request and dropped after it, like the token. It is never stored, cached or logged (product names may appear only in that request's response).
- A household-chosen preferred product (T-052) wins over history when both exist.

## Out of scope
Storing anything from Oda. Prices and offers (T-107). Picking a preferred product by hand (T-052).
