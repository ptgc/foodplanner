## Goal
Architect request 2026-11-05: the weekly groceries (T-092) should have a frequency per item. Bread and milk are bought every week, cheese maybe every second week, other things (e.g. fuel, paper towels) every second week or once a month. "Add weekly groceries" then adds only the items that are due in the week on screen.

## Context
Read docs/domain.md "Weekly groceries (T-092)": model `WeeklyGrocery` (ingredient, optional amount and unit, unique per household and ingredient, at most 50), the button `POST .../shopping-lists/{weekStart}/weekly`, the weekly part on `ShoppingListItem` (`IsWeekly`, `WeeklyAmount`, `WeeklyUnitId`) and `WeeklyGroceries.Plan`/`Overlay` (pure). Non-food items like fuel are own ingredients of the household (rule 5: no free text in the model), in a fitting store section.

## Proposal (see Q-030 for the open points)
- `WeeklyGrocery` gains `Frequency` (`Weekly` | `EveryTwoWeeks` | `Monthly`, default `Weekly` for existing rows) and an anchor (`StartWeek`, a Monday) so "every second week" knows which weeks; due weeks are computed by a pure Domain function (`GroceryFrequency.IsDue(frequency, startWeek, weekStart)`), with unit tests across year ends and 53-week years.
- The button adds only the items due in the week on screen; an item that is not due can still be added by hand from the set ("add anyway").
- Household page: frequency select per item (nb + en), and the next due week shown.
- The UI text changes from "weekly groceries" to "regular groceries" (nb "faste varer").
