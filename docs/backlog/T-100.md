## Goal
Architect request 2026-10-29 (plan only, do not start): "I don't want to go shopping, what can I make?" Suggest recipes for today based on what is in the pantry (T-093) right now. A suggestion for a day, not a shopping list: recipes the household can cook fully from the pantry first, then recipes missing only one or two things (shown as "missing: ...").

## Decisions (Q-029, architect 2026-11-08, option 1 on all)
- Amounts: a pantry entry without an amount means "we have some" and satisfies any amount. With an amount it must cover the recipe amount at the chosen servings (same dimension; mass and volume only convert when the ingredient has a density); a shortfall counts as missing ("missing: 200 g more cheese").
- Show recipes missing at most 2 things (to-taste, as-needed, optional, small frying fat, staples and satisfied alternatives never count; expired pantry entries do not count as at home).
- Ranking: fewest missing first, then the kids' likes (T-026), then not cooked in the last 14 days, then name. The week's meal mix rules (T-090) are not applied; the food type is shown.
- "Cook today" adds the recipe to today's plan in one tap (Adult/Owner; the screen is hidden in kid mode); a second tap adds the missing items to this week's shopping list.
