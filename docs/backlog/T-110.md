## Goal
Architect request 2026-11-05: learn from the shopping lists which things the household buys regularly and suggest them as regular groceries with a frequency (every week, every second week, monthly). Only things added OUTSIDE recipes count: recipes may need the same ingredient almost every week, but that is the recipe, not a regular purchase.

## Context
A shopping list exists per household and week and is kept. Generated items come from the plan's recipes (they have `ShoppingListItemSource` rows and an `IngredientId`); manual items are free text `Label` only (rule 5 allows free text there); weekly items have `IsWeekly` (already regular, so excluded). So the signal is the manual items across past weeks. Because manual items are labels, suggestions group by a normalised label (case, whitespace, diacritics, simple plural) and, when the user accepts one, they pick the ingredient (shared picker, an own ingredient can be created), like the import review.

## Proposal (see Q-030)
- Pure Domain analyser: given the weeks of the last N weeks (default 12) and the normalised labels added by hand in each, suggest a frequency when the label appears often enough and regularly enough (e.g. in at least 4 of 12 weeks; median gap 1 = weekly, 2 = every second week, about 4 = monthly), skipping labels that already match a regular grocery's ingredient name. Thorough unit tests.
- API: `GET .../weekly-groceries/suggestions` (Adult/Owner) returns label, suggested frequency, how many times in how many weeks; dismissing a suggestion hides it (needs storage: see Q-030 point 4).
- Household page: "Suggestions" in the regular groceries card: accept (pick ingredient, frequency preselected, editable) or dismiss.
