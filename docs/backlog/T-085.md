## Goal
Decision Q-019 (option 2): the shopping list follows a usual store layout instead of the alphabet. Every ingredient has a store section, and the "To buy" list is grouped by section in this fixed order, then by name within a section:
1. Fruit and vegetables (Frukt og grønt)
2. Meat, fish and cheese, chilled counter (Kjøtt, fisk og ost)
3. Frozen (Frysevarer)
4. Dairy and juice, chilled shelf: milk, juice, sour cream, yoghurt, eggs (Meieri og juice)
5. Dry goods: pasta, rice, flour, canned, spices, oils, bread and baking (Tørrvarer)
6. Other: manual items and ingredients without a section (Annet)

## Context
Read docs/questions/Q-019.md, docs/domain.md (shopping list T-031/T-076, catalog and translations T-020/T-039, own ingredients and merge T-079, catalog additions T-084), the shopping list route in web/. The section is a property of the ingredient (global seeded rows: set in the seed for every catalog ingredient; own ingredients: chosen on the Ingredients screen, default Other). Display order and the section enum live in Domain (pure, unit tested). The section names are UI text (nb + en), not catalog data.

## Out of scope
Per-household or per-store custom orders and learning the order (T-086); sections for the "Check you have" list beyond keeping it in the same order.
