## Decisions (Q-027, architect 2026-11-08, option 1 on all)
- Refused while the user is the only Owner of a household that has other members ("transfer ownership first", T-048). Households where the user is the only member are deleted with the account, after an explicit confirmation listing them.
- History in households that live on keeps its rows; the link to the person becomes null and the UI shows "Deleted user". Open invites they created are revoked; their recipe import drafts are deleted. Migration.
- Immediate and permanent, after re-entering the password (or a fresh Google sign-in) and typing a confirmation; all sessions signed out. No grace period.
- The delete screen links to the household export (T-044); no personal export.
