## Goal
Two small architect decisions (2026-11-08), both corrections of earlier work:

1. **Monthly suggestions (T-110).** A true monthly item (bought every 4-5 weeks) appears at most 3 times in the 12 weeks the suggester looks at, so the decided "at least 4 of 12" never suggests it. Architect chose option 1: a label seen in **3 of the last 12 weeks** is suggested as Monthly when its gaps are a steady monthly rhythm (about 4 weeks, 4-5 allowed). Weekly and every-second-week keep "at least 4 of 12". A label seen 3 times without a monthly rhythm (e.g. three weeks in a row, then nothing) is not suggested.
2. **rekesaus (T-108).** `rekesaus` is a sauce made with real shrimp, so it counts as `Seafood`, not as a condiment (`None`). Other fish and seafood condiments (fiskesaus, østerssaus, rekepasta, ansjospasta) stay `None`.
