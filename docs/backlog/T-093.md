## Goal
Architect request 2026-10-29: the pantry (T-037) today only remembers confirmed staples. Extend it so the household can register anything it has at home (cheese, tortillas, leftovers from last shopping), optionally with an amount. When the shopping list is generated, it looks at the pantry: what we have covers the need, so only the rest is bought, and covered items ask "check you still have this" instead of going on the buy list.

Example: tacos need 200 g cheese and 8 tortillas. The pantry says cheese 300 g, tortillas (no amount). Result: cheese is not bought, it shows under "Check you have" as "Cheese — 300 g at home (need 200 g)"; tortillas show under "Check you have" as "Tortillas — at home, check there are enough". If the pantry says cheese 100 g, 100 g cheese goes on the buy list, with "100 g at home".

## Context
Read docs/domain.md (Pantry memory T-037, shopping list generation T-031/T-076, unit conversion and QuantityMerger, store sections T-085, weekly groceries T-092, offline cache T-088), docs/backlog/T-037.md. Decisions by the orchestrator (the architect can change them; document them in docs/domain.md):
- `PantryItem` is extended, not replaced: any ingredient (not only staples), optional quantity + unit (structured, rule 5), optional expiry as today. Staples keep working as now (a staple without an amount = "we have it").
- Subtraction is pure Domain logic (`PantryFilter` extended), same dimension only (Mass with Mass, Volume with Volume, Count with Count via the existing conversion); a different dimension or no amount means "have some, check": the item goes to "Check you have" with the pantry note, nothing is bought for it.
- Fully covered → "Check you have" with "X at home (need Y)"; partly covered → buy the rest, show "X at home". The pantry is not reduced automatically when cooking or shopping (out of scope); the user updates or removes entries.
- Adding to the pantry: on the pantry/household screen (ingredient search + optional amount + optional expiry), and from the shopping list ("We have this" on an item, with an optional amount). Checking an item off the shopping list does NOT add it to the pantry (could be a later option).
- Any member may add or update pantry entries (as today); Adult/Owner may clear the whole pantry.
- Weekly groceries (T-092) also subtract the pantry (or are flagged), decide and document.
- The offline shopping cache schema is bumped if the response shape changes (T-088).
