## Goal
Architect request 2026-11-24: once the flows are settled (T-134), give the app a more modern and fresh look that makes people want to use it. It should be led by images and be friendly for kids.

## Context
- Styling today is one hand-written file, `web/app/app.css` (about 570 lines). It has a few tokens on `:root` (`--fg`, `--bg`, `--accent` #1f6f43, `--error`, `--touch` 44px), the system font, light mode only and no dark mode. There is no component library and no Tailwind.
- Kid mode (T-091, T-105) has its own styling. Keep it, or restyle it on purpose.
- The axe check (T-135) runs on the new look, so contrast is checked automatically.
- Rules that stay: 390px mobile-first, touch targets of at least 44px, and the web bundle budget. New fonts or libraries count against the budget.

Open points for the architect. Raise them as a Q-xxx with options when the task starts:
1. Direction: colours, type, how large food images are, and the corner and card style. Mock-ups of 2 or 3 key screens to choose from: week plan, recipe, shopping list.
2. Dark mode now or later.
3. Our own font (it costs bundle size) or the system font.
4. Rollout: tokens first and then screen by screen, or one big change.

## Out of scope
Changes to flow or function. Those belong to T-134.

- [ ] Design direction chosen by the architect (question answered, mock-ups approved)
