How to connect

Connect in three steps

  1. Open Claude settings

    In Claude, on desktop or at claude.ai, open Settings, then Connectors or Integrations, and add an MCP server.

  2. Add the server

    Paste https://oda.com/mcp as the server URL. You do not need an API key.

  3. Sign in to Oda

    Claude sends you to Oda once. Sign in, approve the connection, then ask for groceries in the chat.

  1. Open ChatGPT apps

    In ChatGPT, open Settings, then Apps or Connectors, and add a custom MCP connection.

  2. Paste the server URL

    Paste https://oda.com/mcp as the server URL. You do not need your own API key.

  3. Sign in to Oda

    Sign in to Oda and approve what the connection can do. Then your account is ready in ChatGPT.

  1. Open Gemini settings

    In the Gemini app or Google AI Studio, open the settings for extensions or MCP and add a remote server.

  2. Add the server

    Use https://oda.com/mcp as the server address. You sign in with Oda, not with an API key.

  3. Sign in and approve

    Sign in to Oda when you are asked, and approve the connection. Gemini can then use Oda for you.

  1. Open Cursor MCP settings

    In Cursor, open Settings, then MCP, or edit your MCP config, and add a new remote server.

  2. Add the server

    Set the server URL to https://oda.com/mcp. Cursor finds the tools once you have signed in.

  3. Sign in in your browser

    Cursor opens Oda in your browser the first time. Approve access, go back to Cursor, and ask in Agent mode.

What a chat can do that the website can't

Some jobs need your words, your account and the shop all at once: your agent pulls them together for you, so you don't have to click through every screen yourself.

  • Small swaps, based on how you shop

    Ask your agent to look at your cart alongside your past orders, and it will suggest small, healthier swaps that fit how you already shop, not some generic diet plan.

  • Tidy up your lists

    Tell your agent which lists feel messy, and it can merge the ones that overlap, rename groups and take out things you no longer buy.

  • From guest list to doorstep

    Tell your agent when guests are coming and how many you're expecting, and it can book delivery for before they arrive and fill the cart for that many people.

You stay in control

Your agent can fill the cart, but only you decide what actually gets bought and paid for.

  • You pay in the shop

    Checkout and payment aren't part of MCP, so when your cart is ready your agent simply sends you a link to it: you can review each item, the delivery time and the total, then pay in the shop just as you usually do.

  • Change anything, any time

    Your cart, delivery time and lists stay yours to change, so if something looks wrong you can ask your agent to fix it, or just change it yourself in the shop.

  • You say yes first

    The first time you connect, Oda shows you which client is asking, which account it can use and what it's allowed to do, and you can take that access back at any time.

Questions

What is Oda MCP?

Oda MCP connects your Oda account to an AI agent, so once you're connected it can search for products and recipes, build your cart, see your orders and plan delivery for you.

Which AI tools work with it?

Any client that can connect to a remote MCP server and let you sign in should work: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Cursor and many others. You just paste the server URL and sign in.

Can the agent place an order or pay?

No. There's no checkout or payment in MCP, so your agent sends you a link to your cart and you review and pay at oda.com.

What can the agent see?

Only your Oda account, and only what you approve when you connect, and you can remove that access at any time.

What does it cost?

Nothing extra: it works with your normal Oda account.

Something is not working?

Ask your agent to send us feedback with the feedback tool, and if a tool looks missing or out of date, refresh the connection in your client so it loads the list again.
