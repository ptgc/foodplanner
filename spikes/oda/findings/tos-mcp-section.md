# Oda terms, section "Bruk av Oda via KI-assistenter (MCP)"
Source: https://oda.com/no/legal/betingelser/#bruk-av-oda-via-ki-assistenter-mcp (fetched 2026-10-31)

Original (nb), key bullets: Når du bruker Oda MCP, godtar du:
- å kun bruke den til din egen handel hos Oda, enten privat eller for bedriften din;
- å ikke bruke den til å hente ut, kopiere eller samle inn varer, priser, oppskrifter eller annet innhold fra Oda utover det du trenger for din egen handel;
- å ikke bruke den på vegne av andre, eller videreselge eller gi andre tilgang til den; og
- å ikke omgå bruksgrenser, sikkerhetstiltak eller andre tekniske begrensninger.

English summary:
- Own shopping only (private or for your own company).
- No extracting/copying/collecting products, prices, recipes or other content beyond what the user needs for their own shopping (rules out building our own catalog or price cache from it).
- Not on behalf of others, no resale, no giving others access.
- No circumventing usage limits or technical safeguards.
- "Apps and AI assistants you connect are provided by others than Oda"; the user chooses which apps get access and can revoke it at any time; activity via a connected app counts as the user's own activity. Oda is not responsible for how other apps treat the data.
- Oda may limit or close MCP access and disconnect apps; in serious cases deny the service or deactivate the account.
- Order, payment and account changes are always done by the user in Oda's own app or shop.
